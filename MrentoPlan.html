IQ 200 STRATEGY

Target Audience Insight

Our users are Workers, Laborers, and Thekedars (Contractors). They are busy, practical people who value trust over fancy animations. Many may prefer visual cues and voice interaction over reading complex text.

1. Visual-First Interface (Deewaron Ke Liye)

UI/UX

Iconography & Real Photos

Instead of abstract icons, use real photos of machines (Drill machine, Concrete mixer, Scaffolding). Workers recognize tools by looking at them, not by reading names.

NAV

Bottom Navigation Simplified

Only 3 major tabs: Home (Tools), My Orders, Help. Keep buttons big and tactile for "Mistry" hands.

2. Voice & Vernacular (Aapki Bhasha)

VOICE

"Bol Kar Search Karein"

A big microphone button on the search bar. Workers should be able to say "Mujhe drill machine chahiye" in Hindi/Urdu and see results instantly.

LANG

Hinglish/Urdu Support

The app should speak their language. Instead of "Delivery Status", use "Gadi Kahan Tak Pahunchi".

3. Trust & Reliability (Bharosa)

TRUST

Video Demos

Every tool page should have a 15-30 second video showing:

  • Machine working condition.
  • How to turn it on/off.
  • Safety tips.
HELP

WhatsApp Integration

A direct button to WhatsApp chat. For them, "WhatsApp message = Official Order". It builds instant trust.

4. Financial Simplicity (Hisaab-Kitaab)

  • Clear Pricing: No hidden charges. Show Rent + Security Deposit + Delivery Fee clearly.
  • Refund Tracker: Show exactly when the security deposit will be returned.
  • Easy Invoicing: A simple "download receipt" button that they can show to the main client for reimbursement.

Next Steps: Implementation

  1. Setup Hindi/Urdu Localization.
  2. Integrate Voice-to-Text for search.
  3. Add WhatsApp Support API.
  4. Optimize Image Loading for low-network areas (construction sites).
